Who we are

Trublshut is an African-owned technology company. We think of it as a business technology ecosystem: a parent company with a portfolio of products, each one aimed at an operational problem that African industries deal with every day.

Today the portfolio has two products. Nota54 supports logistics and supply chain work. AYVO is an enquiry response service for businesses that want customer enquiries answered and followed up consistently.

How we build

Start with the operational problem

Each product begins with a specific piece of work that is slow, manual or error-prone inside an industry, such as quoting a shipment or replying to an enquiry.

Keep people in charge

Nota54 flags missing shipment information instead of guessing it. AYVO drafts replies with AI and keeps a person overseeing them.

Say what it does

We describe what each product does in plain terms. We do not promise results we cannot stand behind, and our explainers say when they use sample data.

From first enquiry to booking, in one flow.

Nota54 is Trublshut's product for logistics and supply chain. It takes a customer enquiry, checks the shipment details, compares supplier rates, prepares the quotation and carries an accepted quote into booking preparation.

Nota54 explainer, 51 seconds. Every record shown in the video is sample data.

What the explainer walks through

  1. 1

    Customer inquiry

    An enquiry arrives as a customer email and becomes a record that every later step refers back to.

  2. 2

    Shipment validation

    Origin, destination, equipment, commodity and ready date are pulled from the email. Anything missing, such as dimensions, is flagged.

  3. 3

    Rate comparison

    Supplier rates from rate sheets and emailed quotes are shown side by side with their validity dates. Expired quotes are marked.

  4. 4

    Quotation

    Supplier cost, relevant charges and a configured markup build the customer quotation, shown as a draft before it goes out.

  5. 5

    Customer acceptance

    The customer's reply is linked to the approved quotation, so acceptance is recorded against the right quote.

  6. 6

    Booking preparation

    The accepted quotation moves into booking preparation, still linked to the original inquiry.

A work queue for the team

The explainer ends on a queue that counts new inquiries, quotations awaiting a response and accepted quotations awaiting booking, so it is clear what needs attention next.

Enquiries answered promptly, with people still in charge.

AYVO is an AI-enabled enquiry response service from Trublshut. It audits how your enquiries are handled today, drafts replies with AI under human oversight, follows up on a timer and reports every week.

What the service includes

Enquiry audit

We start by looking at how enquiries reach you and how they are answered now. You receive:

  • A written report
  • A prioritised fix list
  • Reply templates
  • A follow-up sequence

Enquiry response

Replies are drafted by AI and overseen by a person, so every response is checked before the customer relies on it.

Follow-up sequences

Timed follow-ups go out to enquiries that have not moved on, so a first reply is not the last contact.

Weekly reporting

A report each week shows how your enquiries were handled, so you can see what is working and what needs a change.

30-day pilot

We begin with a 30-day pilot on one enquiry source. You see how the service works on your own enquiries before deciding anything wider.

How an enquiry moves through AYVO

  1. 1

    New enquiry

    An enquiry arrives from your chosen source.

  2. 2

    AI-drafted response with human oversight

    AI drafts the reply. A person reviews it.

  3. 3

    Timed follow-up

    A follow-up sequence continues the conversation.

  4. 4

    Weekly reporting

    You receive a weekly report on how enquiries were handled.
